# What's new in The Clawd Files

The changes you'd notice in The Clawd Files universe, newest first. The full, detailed history (every fix, every version) lives in [Clawd Clash](https://github.com/rodclemen/clawd-clash).

## Clawd Clash 3.3.0 (2026-10-15)

- **A new universe.** The Clawd Files joins Star Clawds, Marvelous Clawds, Detective Clawds and Doctor Clawd: pick it in **Settings → Universe**, or in the screen saver's Options.
- **82 characters.** The FBI (Fox Clawder cracking sunflower seeds before pointing at the saucer in the sky, Dana Scrawly holding up her badge, Skinnard rubbing his eyes, Doggedd, Reyez, Einstine, Millar), the Lone Gunclawds, Deep Clawt, Mister X, Marita, Gibson Prays and the Scrawly and Clawder families. Facing them, the Syndicate (the Cigarette-Smoking Clawd and his smoke rings, Clawcek, the Well-Manicured Clawd, Kersh, the Toothpick Man), the aliens (the Bounty Hunter shifting into Scrawly, the Black Oil, the Grey, the Faceless Rebel, the super soldiers) and the monsters of the week, from Eugene Clawms squeezing into a vent to the Great Mutato with his sandwich. HEM turns up in a trench coat with a flashlight.
- **20 places**, from the X-Files office (pencils in the ceiling, I WANT TO BELIEVE) and Skinnard's office in the rain to Bellefleur Woods, Area 51, the Antarctic ice field, Gibsonton and the mothership, each with something moving in it.
- **Its own rides.** The agents leave in an unmarked FBI sedan that drives in along the ground; the Syndicate in a black helicopter; the aliens walk up into a saucer's beam.
- **Missing time.** When one of the seven FBI agents' sessions compacts its context, they're taken up in a beam of light and set back down where they stood, as MISSING TIME types itself out over their head.
- **Ten transitions** built on the show: the Black Oil, a blinking eye, a classified case file, a TV losing sync, a UFO's beam, flashlights in the dark, cigarette smoke, a spy satellite, a paranormal warp, and now and then the whole opening titles.
- **Typed captions.** Every time the scene changes, the new place types itself in at the corner, the way every scene in the show opens: THE X-FILES OFFICE · FBI HEADQUARTERS, WASHINGTON, D.C.
- **Arch-enemies face off first** (Clawder and the Cigarette-Smoking Clawd, Scrawly and Clawms, Skinnard and Clawcek…).
- **Clawd Royale**: The Clawd Files' cast joins everyone else's in one scene when you pick Clawd Royale.
- **A trailer of its own**, at the top of this page.
